Pricing questions

What people actually ask before they book.

If your question is not here, send it through the contact form. The same human who designs the site replies.

Why three tiers instead of one flat site package? +

Because not every business needs the same thing. A solo practitioner who needs a credible web presence fast does not need lead scoring or a chatbot, and a six-figure consultancy cannot afford a brochure site. Three tiers lets you pick the depth of strategy and outcome features that match your actual situation, instead of paying for capability you will never use or settling for capability you do not have.

If none of the three fit, send a note through the contact form and we can scope a custom package.

Can I switch tiers after I have started? +

Yes. Moving up is straightforward. We adjust the scope, you pay the difference, and the extra features get folded into the remaining timeline. Moving down is possible before design starts. After design has begun, the scope lock means a downgrade would mean dropping features already in flight, which is usually not worth the savings.

What is the $499 styling refresh and who is it for? +

The styling refresh is a focused pass on typography, color, spacing, and layout on the site you already have. Nothing gets rebuilt. It is for businesses whose existing site is functional but visually tired, or for owners who want a low-risk way to feel the work before committing to a full package.

If you go on to book Foundation, Growth, or Authority within a few months, your $499 is credited in full toward the package.

What is the Care Plan retainer and what does it cover? +

The Care Plan retainer is the ongoing maintenance package. It covers domain changes, content edits, security updates, and priority support on a monthly subscription. Each tier includes a starting window of retainer time so you are not on your own the day after launch.

Foundation ships with 1 month, Growth with 3 months, Authority with 6 months. After that, the Care Plan is optional and billed monthly. You can stop at any time.

Do you offer payment plans? +

Yes. The default is 50% to book, 50% at launch. For Growth and Authority, three payments (40 / 30 / 30) is available on request. The full amount is locked at booking and does not change mid-project.

What is not included in any tier? +

Hosting and domain. Every site needs them to stay live. They are billed directly to you, run about $10 a month for a small business site, and continue after launch regardless of which tier you choose. You own the domain and the hosting account.

Copywriting and photography. Stock or existing imagery is fine for most projects. If you need professional photography or full copy from scratch, that is a separate engagement and we can refer you to trusted partners.

E-commerce, custom web apps, and complex backends. These are out of scope for the listed tiers. Custom projects are quoted separately.

How many revisions are included? +

Foundation and Growth include 1 to 2 rounds of revisions per page. Authority includes 2 to 3 rounds. The intent is to keep the feedback loop focused: you react to real pages instead of an open-ended comment thread. Extra revision rounds can be added at an hourly rate if a project genuinely needs more iteration.

What happens after the site launches? +

Every tier ships with a post-launch support window (14, 30, or 60 days depending on tier) for bug fixes, training questions, and minor tweaks. After that, the Care Plan retainer covers ongoing changes, or you can handle things in-house through the content management system included with every project.

Do I own the site when it is done? +

Yes. The site, the code, the design files, and the domain are all yours at launch. There is no proprietary platform holding your content hostage. If you want to take the site to another developer or bring maintenance in-house, you can.

What if I am not happy with the result? +

The revision rounds are designed to surface problems early, and there is a 14-day satisfaction checkpoint after launch on every project. If something is genuinely not landing, the work continues until it does, within the scope of the original package. Refunds are handled on a case-by-case basis, but the goal is to ship something you are proud to send people to, not to argue about scope.

Do you build online stores or booking marketplaces? +

Growth includes a scheduling or booking system integration. Authority includes advanced forms and lead routing. Full e-commerce with inventory, payments, shipping, and a custom cart is outside the listed tiers and quoted as a custom project. Send a note through the contact form with what you are selling and we can scope it.

Can I bring my own design or developer? +

Yes. If you have a designer or a developer you want to work with, the build can be handed off in design files or in code at agreed milestones. The project just becomes design-only or build-only at that point, and the price is prorated accordingly.

Will you write the copy and pick the photos? +

The strategy session produces a content outline and recommended page structure. You write the actual copy (or your team does), or we can refer you to a copywriter. For imagery, existing photos and stock work for most projects. Custom photography is a separate engagement.

How long until my site is live? +

Foundation runs 2 to 3 weeks. Growth runs 3 to 5 weeks. Authority runs 5 to 7 weeks. Timelines assume content is provided on a steady cadence. If content takes longer to land, the launch shifts to match. If a project needs to move faster, that can be discussed at booking.

Do you work with agencies or take on white-label projects? +

Occasionally, on a project basis. The agency keeps the client relationship and Resplend Studio handles the build under an NDA. Reach out through the contact form with the project details and we can talk scope.

Is the ADA accessibility baseline really a baseline? +

Yes. Every tier ships with WCAG 2.2 AA contrast, keyboard navigation, screen reader landmarks, focus states, and clean semantic markup. It is the floor, not a premium add-on. Authority includes additional accessibility work for sites in regulated categories, but the foundation is universal.

What if I just want to ask a question first? +

Send a note through the contact form on the home page. The same person who would design your site replies, usually within one business day. No sales scripts, no discovery call required to ask a question.
