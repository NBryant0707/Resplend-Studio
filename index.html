Packages

Three tiers. Every one delivers a complete site.

The difference between tiers is depth of strategy, compliance depth, and outcome-focused features. A resplendent working website is included in every tier.

Choose Foundation if you need a professional presence fast and have a tight budget.

Choose Growth if your site needs to generate real leads — this is where most clients land.

Choose Authority if you need competitive positioning and marketing automation across a larger site.

Ongoing costs: Every website requires hosting and a domain to stay live. These are billed directly to you, cost approximately ~$10/month, and are not included in your project fee. This cost continues after launch regardless of which tier you choose. Optional Care Plan add-ons (below) cover ongoing management and are billed separately.

About Resplend Studio

Built for business owners. Designed to convert.

Most websites look fine but fail to generate actual leads. Resplend Studio builds sites around how your customers make buying decisions, ensuring visitors can easily understand what you offer and take action.

Built Around Your Buyer

Custom layouts planned around the specific questions and objections your clients have before they buy.

Accessible to Every Visitor

Clear contrast, readable layouts, and clean code that keep your site accessible to everyone on any device.

Focused on Measurable Results

Strategic call to action placement, simple booking workflows, and fast loading pages that turn traffic into inquiries.

A great website is not about flashy animations. It comes down to clear positioning, easy navigation, and copy that turns interested visitors into paying clients.

Every project starts with a direct strategy session to map out your audience, your primary services, and the friction points that stop people from reaching out. The structure of the site is built directly from those answers.

Whether you run a solo practice or manage an established team, the focus stays the same: making sure your site clearly communicates your value and makes contacting you effortless.

You work directly with the person building your site from start to finish. There are no account managers, no layers of agency bureaucracy, and no wasted time.
