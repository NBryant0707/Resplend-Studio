About Meridian · est. 2014

A boutique advisory, built for the engagements we wish we'd hired for.

Meridian & Co. was founded in 2014 by four former operators who had spent a decade inside strategy firms and Fortune 500 operating roles. We started Meridian to do one thing well: put senior judgment on the hardest questions facing founder-led companies and private-equity-backed operators — at a price point and engagement model that actually works.

Principles

Five convictions that shape every engagement.

These are not aspirational banners. They are the tradeoffs we've made repeatedly in how we staff, price, and run our work.

01

Senior-led, every engagement.

No junior pyramids. A partner sits in your weekly cadence, with a small bench of senior specialists underneath. We lose some leverage; we gain trust.

02

Embedded operators, not distant strategists.

Our principals are operators first, strategists second. Three of our four founders ran P&Ls of $200M+. We know what the decision you need to make looks like at the weekly business review.

03

Outcomes measured against pre-agreed baselines.

Every engagement has a small set of explicit outcomes negotiated at signing. We are paid against them. Our fees in the years after are the consequence.

04

A small firm on purpose.

We cap the partnership at 18 people. We have turned down billion-dollar firms as acquirers for that reason. The day Meridian is recognizable to your cousin is the day it stops being useful.

05

Patient with principle.

We have turned down work that did not pass our standards, even at the cost of revenue. We can afford to because our partners earned when we ran our own businesses — and we keep our costs flat.

06

Honest about what we don't do.

We don't do regulatory advice, IP litigation strategy, or investment banking. We have a roster of three specialized partners we introduce in those cases — with no referral economics.

The story

From operating benches to advisory benches.

Meridian was founded by four colleagues who met at a Fortune 500 conglomerate in the early 2010s. We had each been frustrated with the same problem from different angles: outside strategy and consulting firms that delivered slide decks but no operating muscle.

We founded the firm with a simple thesis — that mid-market companies and PE-backed operators needed embedded judgment, not episodic decks, and that the right seniority profile was a partner who had run something, not a partner who had advised on something.

The first engagement was a $40M roll-up in the Midwest running out of cash. We embedded for eleven months, rebuilt the operating cadence, and returned it to baseline EBITDA within the year. We have been operating ever since.

Twelve years on, the firm has 14 partners across three offices, ~38 active engagements, and an average tenure of 9 years on the senior team. We do not chase growth. We do not chase rankings. We do not have a press team. We have a roster of clients who call us back, and a steady flow of introductions from them.

2014

Founded in a borrowed conference room.

Four co-founders, one engagement at a time. The first engagement closes the week the firm opens its first checking account.

2017

Chicago office opens. Three partners join.

The second office is opened to support the firm's growing PE operating partner engagements. Three senior operators are recruited from industry.

2019

The framework is formalized.

The four-stage operating model is written down for the first time after 60+ engagements. It will be revised eighteen times in the next six years.

2021

The London office opens.

A London outpost serves as our European hub — managing 11 European clients and supporting cross-Atlantic PE deals.

2024

Two more partners join. Practice grows to six.

The first dedicated digital and sustainability practice partners join the firm. The framework is rebuilt to accommodate capital-structure work.

2026

Where we are today.

14 partners, 38 active engagements, 94% client retention across rolling three-year windows, 4.8× median EBITDA uplift on completed transformations.
