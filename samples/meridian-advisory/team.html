The Meridian team

Fourteen partners, each running their own engagement.

The senior team at Meridian is a deliberately small bench — fourteen partners and ten senior associates across three offices. Median tenure is nine years; most arrived from operating roles at companies you've heard of. Every engagement you see at Meridian is led from start to finish by the partner named here.
