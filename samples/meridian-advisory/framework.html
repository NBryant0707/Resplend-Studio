Strategic framework

How an engagement actually runs.

A four-stage operating model refined across 200+ engagements over twelve years. It is not a marketing artifact. Every active engagement at Meridian runs against this framework — the weekly business reviews, the partner cadence, the dashboards, the 90-day post-engagement check-ins are the same on every deal. Below is the version we use internally.

01

Diagnostic.

2 weeks $40K–$90K On-site, full-time

Two weeks on-site with the senior team. We build a written view of the situation before any solution work begins.

Two weeks on-site. No slides, no preamble.

Our partners sit beside your operators for ten business days. We don't bring a stack of templates — we bring structured questions, calibrated judgment, and a willingness to find the answer you don't want to hear.

By the end of week two we deliver a written diagnostic memo. Twelve pages, plain prose, the kind the leadership team will actually read. It is the document everyone reads at the executive team meeting that follows.

Stakeholder coverage

  • 12–20 structured interviews across functions
  • One-on-ones with the executive team (90 min each)
  • Frontline ride-alongs (manufacturing floor, support queues, sales calls)
  • Customer interviews where the relationship allows

Financial teardown

  • Revenue cohort analysis and pricing architecture
  • Unit economics by segment, customer, and channel
  • Working-capital review — DSO/DIO/DPO deep-dives
  • Capex history review and capital deployment scorecard

Operating view

  • Org design and span-of-control mapping
  • Incentive alignment — comp plans, KPIs, performance cycles
  • Productivity and capacity assessment
  • Customer and vendor voice — interviews and ride-alongs

What you receive

  • A 12-page diagnostic memo the executive team actually reads
  • A data appendix with the underlying cohort and unit-economics views
  • A pre-read deck for the executive team meeting
  • Our preliminary read on where the next stage should focus

02

Strategy.

6 weeks $120K–$220K Hybrid cadence

The diagnostic synthesizes into a strategy & 18-month operating plan — three scenarios, defensible at the board, ready to execute.

Three scenarios. One decision.

The strategy stage synthesizes the diagnostic into an 18-month operating plan with three financial scenarios — base, stretch, and transformational — each tied to specific capital, hiring, and capability decisions.

Every scenario is stress-tested against the same set of operating decisions: where do we deploy capital, who do we hire, what do we stop doing, what do we start, and what is the threshold at which the next scenario becomes the right one. Our partners sit in the boardroom while this is debated, not after.

Three scenarios, modeled in parallel

  • Base — what the existing plan looks like, optimized
  • Stretch — one or two strategic bets layered on top
  • Transformational — full operating-model redesign with sequenced capital

Modeling

  • Full P&L with revenue, gross margin, opex, and capex
  • Headcount plan with timing and skill requirements
  • Cash flow waterfall with debt covenants tracked
  • Sensitivity analysis on the assumptions that matter

Roadmap

  • Quarterly milestones with named owners
  • Sequenced hiring and capital deployment calendar
  • Decision gates — what forces a re-look vs. continue
  • Risk register with mitigation owners

Board packaging

  • 30-page board deck, defensible under cross-examination
  • Three-scenario financial summary page
  • Capital structure and financing options memo
  • The board pre-read we hand to the chair the day before

What you receive

  • The 18-month operating plan, three scenarios
  • The capital structure memo
  • The board-ready strategy deck
  • The transition plan for entering the execution stage

03

Execution.

6–9 months $1.4M–$3.6M Embedded operator

Senior Meridian staff sit in your operating cadence for six to nine months. We are accountable to the same metrics you are.

Embedded operators. Measurable outcomes.

The execution stage is where the strategy actually runs. Senior Meridian staff sit in your operating cadence — weekly business reviews, monthly steering committees, quarterly board prep — for the duration of the engagement. We are accountable to the same metrics you are.

We do not staff this from across the bench. One partner is named for the engagement. They are the one in the operating cadence. The senior specialists underneath them rotate through as the work demands — but the partner stays.

Operating partner assigned

  • One partner named for the engagement, end-to-end
  • Sits in your executive team meetings weekly
  • Holds decision rights alongside your COO/CFO
  • Direct line to your board chair on capital reallocation

Cadence

  • Weekly business reviews with structured dashboards
  • Monthly steering committee with capital reallocation rights
  • Quarterly board prep with the chair ahead of the meeting
  • Real-time tracking against the strategy stage milestones

Workstreams

  • Sequenced by the strategy stage; budget ring-fenced
  • Named workstream leads, accountable for outcomes
  • Weekly workstream syncs feeding the WBR
  • Explicit decision-rights matrix per workstream

What you receive

  • Weekly WBR with structured action register
  • Monthly steering committee memo and minutes
  • Mid-engagement strategy refresh (month 4)
  • Real-time dashboards with operating and financial metrics

04

Embed.

12 weeks Incl. in execution 90-day check-in built-in

Twelve weeks to independence. The operating muscle stays with your team, supported by measurement infrastructure and a structured hand-off.

Twelve weeks to independence.

The embed stage is where we hand the operating muscle back to your team. The dashboards, the cadence, the workstream structure — all of it stays. We train your team to run it, document it, and improve it without us in the room.

The 90-day check-in is built in, written into the engagement letter. We come back at day +90, walk the metrics against the strategy-stage baseline, and make a recommendation on whether to re-engage. Three of every five engagements re-engage in some form, mostly at a smaller scope.

Measurement infrastructure

  • Dashboard and KPI architecture, fully documented
  • Weekly business review runbook (note templates, decision-rights)
  • Monthly steering committee structure and pre-read template
  • Alerting and exception handling thresholds

Leadership coaching

  • Top 8 executives receive structured coaching
  • Monthly 1:1 cadence, written coaching notes
  • Succession planning support for two key positions
  • Performance calibration support during the hand-off

Documentation playbook

  • Each workstream has a written operations manual
  • Decision-rights matrix is mapped to role definitions
  • Process documentation lives where the team works
  • Onboarding materials for new joiners

90-day check-in

  • Written re-engagement option, included in the engagement letter
  • Walk-through of metrics vs. the strategy-stage baseline
  • Independent assessment of operating muscle strength
  • Pre-agreed scope and price for re-engagement
The cadence inside an engagement

Eight meetings your calendar will have.

The same cadence on every Meridian engagement, regardless of practice area. Predictability is part of what we charge for.

Weekly business review

Mondays · 90 min · Operating team. The dashboard is reviewed against the strategy-stage baseline; action register is closed out.

Operating partner 1:1

Weekly · 30 min · CEO or COO. Relationship check-in, decision escalated before the WBR.

Workstream sync

Twice weekly · 60 min · Workstream lead + specialists. Hands-on execution, blockers cleared.

Steering committee

Monthly · 3 hr · Cross-functional. Capital reallocation, scope changes, cross-workstream issues.

Board prep

Quarterly · 90 min · Chair + CEO pre-read. Operating partner walks the board narrative in advance.

Strategy refresh

Month 4 · 1 day · Offsite. Mid-engagement re-look at scenarios — does the base case hold or do we shift?

Embed hand-off

Weeks 1–4 of embed stage. Cadence handed to the team with runbooks, training, and named owners.

90-day check-in

Built into the engagement letter. Walk the metrics, write the recommendation, decide on re-engagement.
