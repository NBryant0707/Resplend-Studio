Strategic framework

How an engagement actually runs.

A four-stage operating model refined across 200+ client engagements. Read the full framework →

  1. 01Diagnostic

    Two weeks on-site. Stakeholder interviews, financial teardown, customer ride-alongs. You leave with a single memo the leadership team actually reads.

  2. 02Strategy

    Six weeks synthesizing the diagnostic into a strategy & 18-month operating plan. Three scenario paths, board-ready, defensible.

  3. 03Execution

    Embedded operators alongside your team. Weekly cadence, monthly steering committee. We measure what we said we would.

  4. 04Embed

    Twelve-week hand-off with measurement infrastructure, leadership coaching, and a 90-day post-engagement check-in built in.

Diagnostic · Week 01–02

Two weeks on-site. No slides, no preamble.

Our partners sit beside your operators for ten business days. We don't bring a stack of templates — we bring structured questions, calibrated judgment, and a willingness to find the answer you don't want to hear.

  • Structured stakeholder interviews across 12–20 functions
  • Customer & supplier ride-alongs with field notes
  • Working capital, unit economics, and revenue cohort teardown
  • A 12-page diagnostic memo the executive team actually reads
Strategy · Week 03–08

Three scenarios. One decision.

The strategy stage synthesizes the diagnostic into an 18-month operating plan with three financial scenarios — base, stretch, and transformational — each tied to specific capital, hiring, and capability decisions.

  • Three-scenario financial model with full P&L, capex, and headcount
  • 18-month operating roadmap with quarterly milestones
  • Capital structure and financing options memorandum
  • Board-ready strategy deck, defensible under cross-examination
Execution · Month 03–09

Embedded operators. Measurable outcomes.

Senior Meridian staff sit in your operating cadence — weekly business reviews, monthly steering committee, quarterly board prep. We are accountable to the same metrics you are.

  • Operating partner assigned to your executive team
  • Weekly business reviews with structured dashboards
  • Monthly steering committee with capital reallocation rights
  • Real-time tracking against the strategy stage milestones
Embed · Month 10–12

Twelve weeks to independence.

The embed stage hands the operating muscle to your team with measurement infrastructure, leadership coaching, and a 90-day post-engagement check-in to confirm the gains hold.

  • Measurement infrastructure (dashboards, KPI definitions, alerts)
  • Leadership coaching for top 8 executives
  • Documentation playbook your team runs without us
  • 90-day post-engagement check-in with re-engagement option
