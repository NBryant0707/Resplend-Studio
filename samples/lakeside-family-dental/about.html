About the practice

A practice built on listening.

Eighteen years serving [City] families. Same idea since day one: dental visits shouldn't feel rushed, transactional, or scary.

Dr. Sarah Reed opened Lakeside Family Dental in 2006 with a simple idea: dental visits shouldn't feel rushed, transactional, or scary. After graduating from the University of Michigan School of Dentistry and completing a general practice residency at the Cleveland Clinic, she returned to her hometown to build the kind of practice she'd want to bring her own family to. Eighteen years later, that idea still shapes everything we do - from how we schedule appointments (longer slots, fewer patients per day) to how we explain treatment plans (no jargon, no pressure, written estimates you can take home).

We've grown to five dentists and a support team of twenty, but we still operate like a small practice. You'll see the same hygienist at every cleaning. We'll remember your kid's name. And we'll never recommend treatment you don't need. Our clinical team includes Dr. Sarah Reed (founder, general dentistry), Dr. James Okafor (oral surgery and implants), Dr. Priya Sharma (pediatric dentistry), Dr. Marcus Chen (orthodontics and Invisalign), and Dr. Elena Vasquez (cosmetic and restorative). All five are [State]-licensed and complete 40+ hours of continuing education every year.

Our facility was designed with accessibility in mind from day one: step-free entrances, accessible parking near the front door, wide hallways, accessible operatories with height-adjustable chairs, and a quiet sensory-friendly room for patients who need a calmer environment. Our staff is trained in trauma-informed care and we're experienced with patients who have autism, dementia, mobility limitations, and dental anxiety. We believe good dental care should be available to everyone in our community - not just those who can navigate a typical dental office.

Beyond the practice, we're committed to our community. Dr. Reed volunteers monthly at the [City] Community Health Center dental clinic. Every February (National Children's Dental Health Month) we host a free dental day where kids in need get cleanings, sealants, and fluoride treatments at no cost. Last year we provided over $40,000 in free care and treated 180 children. We also partner with three local elementary schools on an in-classroom dental education program.

We're not the cheapest dental office in [City], and we're not trying to be. We're the office where you don't feel rushed, where the recommendations make sense, where your kids like coming, and where you trust that what we're recommending is what we'd recommend for our own families. If that resonates, we'd love to be your dental home.

Come see what we're about.

The best way to know if we're the right fit is a no-pressure first visit. We'll give you an honest assessment, a written plan, and time to think it over.
