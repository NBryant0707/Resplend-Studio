Frequently asked

Questions new patients often ask.

We get a lot of the same questions from new patients. Here are honest answers - if yours isn't covered, just call or send us a note and we'll get back to you the same business day.

Do you accept my insurance?

We accept most major dental insurance plans, including Delta Dental, MetLife, Cigna, Aetna, Guardian, UnitedHealthcare, and most [State] Medicaid plans. We'll verify your specific plan before your first visit and give you a clear written estimate of any out-of-pocket costs. If we don't accept your insurance, we'll tell you upfront - and we offer an in-house membership plan that may save you money.

What if I have dental anxiety?

You're not alone - about a third of our patients experience some level of dental anxiety. We offer longer appointment times, noise-cancelling headphones, nitrous oxide, oral sedation, and a calm quiet room. Tell us when you book and we'll plan accordingly. There's never any judgment here.

Is the facility accessible?

Yes - fully. Step-free entrance, accessible parking near the front door, wide hallways, accessible operatories with height-adjustable chairs, and accessible restrooms. Our office was purpose-built for accessibility, not retrofitted. If you have specific needs, please call ahead and we'll prepare.

How quickly can I get an appointment?

New patient cleanings are typically scheduled within 2-3 weeks. Emergency same-day appointments are available - call our main line and select option 1. If you're in pain, we'll see you today.

Do you treat children?

Yes - we see patients of all ages, including children. Our pediatric room is designed for kids, and three of our dentists specialize in pediatric care. First visits for children under 3 are complimentary.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. We offer 0% interest payment plans for treatments over $1,000 through CareCredit and our in-house plan. Most cosmetic and implant work can be spread over 6-18 months. We'll lay out all financing options at your consultation before you commit to anything.

What if I have a dental emergency after hours?

Our after-hours line is staffed by an on-call dentist (not an answering service) for true emergencies - severe pain, knocked-out tooth, facial swelling, or uncontrolled bleeding. Call the main number and follow the prompts. Non-urgent messages left after hours will be returned the next business day.

What happens at my first visit?

Your first visit is 60-90 minutes. We take a full medical and dental history, complete X-rays and a comprehensive exam, and do a cleaning if time allows. You'll leave with a written treatment plan (if any treatment is recommended) and clear pricing. There's no pressure to schedule follow-up work before you're ready.

What sedation options do you offer?

We offer three levels of sedation: nitrous oxide (laughing gas, wears off in minutes and you can drive yourself home), oral conscious sedation (a pill you take before your appointment, requires someone to drive you), and IV sedation for longer procedures. All three are administered by dentists with separate sedation certifications. We'll discuss options at your consultation based on your anxiety level and the procedure.

Still have a question we didn't cover?

Call us during business hours and a real person (not a phone tree) will pick up. We're happy to answer anything - big or small - before you book.
