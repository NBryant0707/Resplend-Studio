Services

Comprehensive care under one roof.

From preventive cleanings to full-mouth restoration, our team handles nearly every dental need in-house - so you rarely need a referral to a specialist.

General & Preventive

Routine cleanings, comprehensive exams, fluoride treatments, sealants, and oral cancer screenings - recommended every six months for most patients. We use digital X-rays (about 90% less radiation than traditional film) and intraoral cameras so you can see exactly what we see on a chairside screen. The foundation of a healthy smile and the best way to catch small issues before they become expensive ones.

Schedule a cleaning →

Cosmetic

Professional whitening (in-office and take-home trays), porcelain veneers, composite bonding, and full smile makeovers. We offer a complimentary digital smile preview so you can see potential results on your own face before committing to any treatment. Most cosmetic work completes in 2-3 visits, and we'll provide written pricing upfront.

Explore cosmetic options →

Restorative

Crowns, bridges, dental implants, root canals, and full-mouth restoration. We have a CEREC machine on-site for same-day crowns - no temporary crown, no second visit, no extra time off work. Implant consultations are complimentary, and we'll explain all your options (including non-implant alternatives) before recommending a treatment path.

Discuss restoration →

Orthodontics

Traditional metal braces, clear ceramic braces, and Invisalign clear aligners for teens and adults. Three of our dentists are Invisalign-certified, and we include retainers in the treatment cost (most offices charge extra). Complimentary consultations include a digital scan, treatment timeline, and payment plan options - no commitment required.

Book a consultation →

Emergency Care

Same-day emergency appointments are available Monday through Saturday for both existing and new patients. Call our main line and select option 1 to reach our emergency desk directly - you'll speak with a person, not an answering service. For after-hours emergencies, our recorded message includes the on-call dentist's pager number.

Call for emergencies →

Pediatric Dentistry

A dedicated pediatric room with toys, ceiling-mounted TVs, and a team trained in gentle kid-specific techniques. We follow the 'first tooth, first visit' guideline from the American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry - children should see a dentist by age 1. First visits for children under 3 are complimentary, and we never restrain or force treatment.

Schedule for your child →
