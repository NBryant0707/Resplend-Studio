From our patients

What people say after their visit.

Choosing a dentist is personal. Here's what real patients say after spending time in our chairs - the good, the calm, the occasionally funny. Every quote is from an actual patient who agreed to share their experience (with permission, last names abbreviated).

"I've avoided the dentist for ten years because of anxiety. The team here was patient, kind, and never made me feel guilty. I actually look forward to my appointments now."

Sarah M., patient since 2022

"My kids (ages 5, 8, and 11) all love coming here. The pediatric room has toys and a TV on the ceiling. The dentists explain everything in language they understand."

James R., patient since 2019

"I use a wheelchair and most dental offices are impossible. Lakeside was built for accessibility - I can get right up to the chair. That matters more than people realize."

Maria K., patient since 2020

"I'd been embarrassed by my smile for fifteen years. Dr. Vasquez walked me through every option - veneers, bonding, whitening - without pressure. I chose veneers on my front four teeth. The result is so natural my own mother didn't notice until I told her."

Linda T., patient since 2023

"I cracked a tooth on a Friday afternoon and was in serious pain. They squeezed me in within two hours of my call, fixed the tooth that same day, and texted me over the weekend to check how I was healing. That's not normal. That should be normal."

David K., patient since 2021

"At 72, I expected to lose more teeth. Dr. Okafor laid out a clear plan for the two implants I needed, worked with my Medicare Advantage plan, and finished in four visits. I have my smile back. I'm eating steak again."

Robert H., patient since 2018
