The Atelier

From paper to wrist,
eleven days.

01 · The Drawing Board

Every calibre begins as a single line on paper. Our master horologer, Marc Aubert, drafts each component by hand — 312 pieces, drawn over twelve weeks.

02 · Case Milling

Grade 5 titanium, milled from a single billet over 47 hours. No two cases share the exact grain.

03 · Hand Assembly

Each watch is assembled by one watchmaker, named on the caseback. They spend eleven days with a single movement.

04 · Regulation

Adjusted to within ±2 seconds per day, across six positions, over fourteen days.

Marc Aubert, Maître Horloger
